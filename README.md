# AI Resume Analyzer

An intelligent application that analyzes your CV/resume and suggests jobs based on your skills, with a job search feature to find recent jobs and internships by country.

## Features

- Upload CV/Resume (PDF format)
- AI-powered skill extraction from resume
- Job suggestions based on your skills
- Search jobs by country
- Find recent jobs and internships
- Direct links to job postings
- Country-based job filtering

## How to Use

### Resume Analysis
1. Upload your CV/Resume file
2. AI analyzes your skills automatically
3. View suggested jobs matching your skills

### Job Search
1. Click the Search Jobs button
2. Select a country from the dropdown
3. View recent jobs and internships in that country
4. Click links to apply directly
